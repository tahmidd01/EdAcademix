About

The University of Padua, founded in 1222, is one of Europe’s most historic universities, situated in Padua, Italy. It is celebrated for its achievements in medicine, astronomy, and law, with a legacy of influential scholars like Galileo Galilei. Combining a rich academic heritage with innovative research, the university continues to be a leading institution in Europe, fostering knowledge and discovery in various fields.
